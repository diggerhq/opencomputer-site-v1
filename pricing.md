# OpenComputer Pricing

Elastic memory and CPU, resizable at runtime. Every VM includes 20 GB of disk. You pay only while the VM is running.

## On-Demand Compute

| Memory | CPU | Per Minute | Per Hour | Per Month |
|--------|--------|-----------|----------|-----------|
| 1 GB | 1 vCPU | $0.001 | $0.06 | $42.18 |
| 4 GB | 1 vCPU | $0.004 | $0.24 | $168.72 |
| 8 GB | 2 vCPU | $0.008 | $0.48 | $337.44 |
| 16 GB | 4 vCPU | $0.016 | $0.96 | $674.88 |

## Storage

- 20 GB of disk is included with every VM.
- Disk above the included 20 GB is metered at $0.0000001 / GB-second (about $0.26 / GB-month), billed for the lifetime of the sandbox, whether running or hibernated.

## Notes

- VMs can hibernate when not in use and wake at any point, with state preserved. Compute charges apply only while running.
- Memory and CPU can be resized while the VM is running.
